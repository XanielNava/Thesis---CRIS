About CRIS

Community-Centric Rabies Intelligence System

Community-Centric Rabies Intelligence System

CRIS is a web-based Provincial Rabies Surveillance and Decision Support System designed to assist the Provincial Health Office in monitoring animal bite cases, human rabies deaths, animal rabies deaths, epidemiological investigations, and hotspot analysis throughout Iloilo Province.
